June 4, 2025 · 2 min

What Do I Want to Code?#

  1. Something that excites me and that I’m genuinely passionate about.
  2. Something that can be iterated and tinkered on for months or years.
  3. Something that strikes multiple domains, not just coding / learning code.
  4. Something that genuinely forces me to think technically.

After considering all of these, a game engine is just an obvious choice.

I’ve always been a gamer. I want to go back to my C++ roots, but in the most hardcore of ways.
I want to do something difficult.

I’ll try to post what I’ve already done in my next post.

About Me (feel free to skip)#

I started this blog to document what I’ve learned and built so far while working on my own game engine.

Right now, we’re in the beginning phases, so I’m just writing a little about what I’m doing and why I’m doing it.

Like many developers, I grew up as a gamer — so that’s no surprise. I went to university, got my degree, and worked for a few years in .NET (C#), first at an ERP software company, then at the largest trading firm in Philadelphia.

  • Smart and kind coworkers
  • Free lunch
  • Happy hours
  • Phillies games
  • Great pay

…and then I was let go last August.

Oof.
It is what it is.

Since then, I’ve been unemployed — but not stagnant. I went on a month-long excursion abroad, proposed, got married, and have been thriving in other parts of life.

Now, it’s time to get back into coding — but the idea of grinding through LeetCode/HackerRank puzzles makes my skin crawl.

C’est la vie.
